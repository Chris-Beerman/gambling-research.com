Topic rules

A study is tagged with a topic when its title or abstract matches the patterns below (case-insensitive regular expressions) often enough: a match in the title counts twice, a match in the abstract once, and the topic needs the number of matches in the second column. The same rules are in config/taxonomy.yml in the repository, where anyone can propose a change.

Design rules

For PubMed records, the publication types assigned by NLM's indexers decide whether a study is a trial, a review, a protocol, a commentary or a case report; the patterns below decide the rest, and decide everything for studies outside PubMed. The title patterns apply to the title; the abstract patterns are phrased to catch authors describing their own study rather than studies they cite. Where two designs cannot both be true, the first one listed wins: a protocol is not also shown as a trial, and a review or commentary is not also shown as a survey.
