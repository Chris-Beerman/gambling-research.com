The week's shortlist is heavy on data that gamblers did not supply themselves: account records from an online sportsbook, Swedish prescription and diagnosis registers, NHS clinic referrals mapped against venue locations, and Statistics Canada's 2025 gambling module. In policy, the CFTC gave prediction markets 30 days to remove state geofences in the same week a federal judge ordered Kalshi's sports contracts off two California tribes' lands.

## Studies worth reading

### [Multidimensional loss chasing among online sports gamblers: Assessing the moderating effects of starting behaviors on harm prediction](https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2026.00113)

*Journal of Behavioral Addictions, United States.*

Edson, Tom and LaPlante of the Division on Addiction at Cambridge Health Alliance, following their 2025 paper that tested bet size, odds and time-between-bets as loss-chasing markers in bwin and DraftKings records. The abstract opens by asking whether the link between loss chasing and harm depends on overall involvement, such as average stake and number of bets. Free full text.]

### [Gambling venues and their association with incidence of help-seeking gambling disorder: A Bayesian spatial analysis](https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2025.00483)

*Journal of Behavioral Addictions, United Kingdom.*

Ioannidis, Solly, Chamberlain and colleagues, using referrals to NHS-led gambling services; the abstract says no published data from those services have yet examined physical access to venues. A paper by the same group with almost the same title appeared in BJPsych Open in 2025, so check whether this is a new analysis before covering it. [TO VERIFY: which clinics and years, how incidence was measured, which venue types were associated with incidence, effect sizes, limitations, funding. Free full text.]

### [Missing the target: Young adults were wrongfully targeted by gambling ads on social media despite UK and Dutch regulations](https://doi.org/10.1111/add.70638)

*Addiction, United Kingdom and the Netherlands.*

Xiao, Deery, Tattan-Birch and colleagues, on gambling advertising aimed at under-18s and at 18- to 24-year-olds, whom the abstract describes as permitted to gamble but more vulnerable to harm than older adults. The method is presumably Meta's ad repository, as in the group's earlier Dutch and Belgian papers, but that has not been confirmed. [TO VERIFY: number of adverts, operators, targeting settings found, estimated reach of young people, limitations, funding. Free full text.]

### [Gambling disorder and other impulse control disorders associated with pramipexole: a nationwide Swedish case-control study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-026-08661-2)

*BMC Psychiatry, Sweden.*

Lecerof, Wolfschlag and Håkansson, on the dopamine agonist prescribed for restless legs and Parkinson's disease that has been linked to gambling disorder and other impulse control disorders as a side effect. The group's register-based cohort in Journal of Affective Disorders earlier this year found the same association in people with psychiatric disorders. [TO VERIFY: numbers of cases and controls, registers and years, odds ratios for gambling disorder and for other impulse control disorders, limitations, funding. Free full text.]

### [Sociodemographic and Behavioural Predictors of Gambling and Problematic Gambling in Canada: Evidence from the 2025 CCHS](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10899-026-10573-5)

*Journal of Gambling Studies, Canada.*

Williams, Shaw, Allami, Hodgins and colleagues, using the Rapid Response Gambling Module of the 2025 Canadian Community Health Survey, administered to a representative sample of 16,351 adults in the ten provinces between January and July 2025. This appears to be the same data behind the Alberta Gambling Research Institute estimate, reported by CBC in October, that nearly 130,000 Ontarians show signs of problem gambling. [TO VERIFY: prevalence of gambling and problem gambling, which activities and demographics predicted problems, whether online and sports betting stand out, limitations, funding. Paywalled.]

### [Did legalized sports betting hurt Las Vegas's tourism?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11149-026-09532-0)

*Journal of Regulatory Economics, United States.*

Meehan and Stephenson, on whether the spread of legal sports betting to 38 states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico after Murphy v. NCAA drew visitors away from Las Vegas. ## Policy and public health news

- [CFTC gives prediction markets 30 days to remove geographic restrictions in the U.S.](https://cdcgaming.com/brief/cftc-gives-prediction-markets-30-days-to-remove-geographic-restrictions-in-the-u-s/) A November 5 directive under a new reading of the impartial-access rules tells designated contract markets to drop domestic geofences within 30 days or explain why they cannot; courts have ordered Kalshi to restrict sports contracts in Michigan, Nevada and Washington.
- [California tribes win injunction to block Kalshi sports contracts on their land](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/?p=286051) Judge Jacqueline Scott Corley granted the injunction on November 4 after the Ninth Circuit sent the case back; the tribes must supply maps of the affected lands by November 13 and Kalshi must report on compliance by November 25.
- [KalshiEX LLC v. Williams](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73601450/kalshiex-llc-v-williams/) (Second Circuit, November 6): amicus briefs filed by the North American Gaming Regulators Association with the International Association of Gaming Regulators, and by Better Markets, in Kalshi's appeal against the New York Gaming Commission; the appellees' brief was filed on October 30.
- [Massachusetts: Proxy betting, limiting violations on regulatory agenda](https://cdcgaming.com/massachusetts-proxy-betting-limiting-violations-on-regulatory-agenda/) The Gaming Commission will hold a hearing on 22 wagers totalling $21,800 placed through Bill Simmons's FanDuel account by another person, and a separate hearing on Fanatics' alleged failure to notify 132 limited patrons under the state's 48-hour explanation rule.
- [GC says illegal gambling websites used by 2.5% of UK online gamblers but GCI questions survey method](https://igamingbusiness.com/?p=487641) First-year figures from the Gambling Survey for Great Britain (14,257 respondents, 3,569 past-year online gamblers): 2.5 percent of online gamblers used an illegal site in the past four weeks, 0.6 percent of all adults; Gambling Compliance International says a user count is not a revenue share.
- [Canada sports betting advertising bill advances after House committee approves amendments](https://www.casino.org/news/canadian-gaming-sports-betting-advertising-bill-advances/) The Heritage committee reported Bill S-211 on November 3 with the national framework deadline extended from one year to two and consultation made optional, and rejected a Conservative amendment to ban in-game betting ads; third reading is next, with the House sitting until December 11.
- [Nevada: U.S. Rep. Dina Titus urges Congress to pass gambling tax change](https://cdcgaming.com/nevada-u-s-rep-dina-titus-urges-congress-to-pass-gambling-tax-change/) A letter to the Speaker and Majority Leader asks for the FAIR BET Act (H.R. 4304), which would restore the full gambling-loss deduction cut to 90 percent last year, to be attached to must-pass legislation in the session beginning November 9; it cleared Ways and Means 38-5 in September.
- [Sporttrade secures CFTC registrations to launch prediction market business](https://www.covers.com/industry/sporttrade-secures-cftc-registrations-to-launch-prediction-market-business-nov-3-2026) The former New Jersey sportsbook, which left five state markets in June, announced designated contract market and clearing organisation registrations on November 3; the CFTC's [order of designation](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/documents/2026/orgdcmstorderofdesignsi261102.pdf) names Sporttrade DCM LLC of Camden.

## Trials and preregistrations

- [Low-Intensity Focused Ultrasound for Gambling Disorder](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07923032) ClinicalTrials.gov, not yet recruiting, University of Virginia with NaviFUS Corporation; a sham-controlled device trial in 54 treatment-seeking adults, due to start in February 2027, testing whether focused ultrasound is safe and reduces gambling symptoms.
- [Loss-Chasing in Online Sports Betting Markets: Causal Evidence from West Virginia](https://osf.io/bgyh2/) OSF Registries, registered November 7, no sponsor listed; a preregistration by Brad Humphreys and colleagues at West Virginia University of a study of individual-level loss chasing in the account data that state law requires West Virginia operators to share with the university, funded by Arnold Ventures according to the [university's August announcement](https://wvutoday.wvu.edu/stories/2026/08/11/wvu-research-aims-to-curb-risky-loss-chasing-in-online-betting).
